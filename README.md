# Fleet Span University — demo

A clickable demo of a custom training app with sample people and data. It has no real sign-in, no database and no emails; everything resets on reload.

## Put it on GitHub Pages

1. Sign in to GitHub and click **New repository**.
2. Name it `fleet-span-university-demo`. Leave the other settings as they are and click **Create repository**.
3. On the new repository page, click **uploading an existing file**. Drag in `index.html` and `README.md`, then click **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings** → **Pages**.
5. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to **Deploy from a branch**. Set **Branch** to **main** and the folder to **/ (root)**, then click **Save**.
6. Wait a minute or two, then refresh the Pages settings. The site address appears at the top: `https://<your-username>.github.io/fleet-span-university-demo/`

## Before you share the link

- On a free GitHub account, Pages sites are public. Anyone with the link can open the demo, and the repository's files are public too. The page tells search engines not to list it, but that does not make it private.
- Everything in it is sample data. Keep real employee names, scores and shop policies out of this copy.

## Walking through the demo

Use **Viewing as** at the top to switch between Admin, Service Manager, Reviewer and Tech. **Reset demo** puts the sample data back.

1. As the Tech, open "Diagnose button on the SO", enter an SO number, and take the quiz.
2. As Service Manager Marcus, open Team progress to see the team and act on anyone who needs in-person training.
3. As Admin, open Dashboard, Modules (with the quiz preview and answers), Reminders, Roster and Audit log.
